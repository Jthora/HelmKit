# Can psionics be worked out without new experiments? An audit of the wiki's "Effective Physics" framing

**Status**: v0, 2026-08-06. Research finding from live wiki browsing (not curl — WAF-safe browser fetch), triggered by the question: since the wiki files psionics under "Effective Physics" rather than treating ψ as a foundational field, is there real theoretical leverage to make progress without running new HelmKit experiments?

## Short answer

Partially, and the boundary is precise, not fuzzy. There's a real, sizeable chunk of test-free work available — but it's narrower than "derive whether ψ exists." What's available: (1) a mainstream-adjacent sub-theory (CEMI) that's already testable against **existing, already-published** neuroscience data, no new experiment required; (2) a real methodology, borrowed by the wiki's own analogy, for bounding the ψ-specific coupling using **existing precision data collected for other purposes**, the way particle physics bounds axion couplings; (3) several of the framework's own stated predictions that are checkable against **existing public datasets** (parapsychology meta-analyses, consciousness-research PCI data) rather than new HelmKit sessions. None of this can prove the ψ-specific part is real — that ceiling is structural, not a gap in effort, and the wiki's own analogy explains why.

## What "Effective Physics" actually is on the wiki

Fetched the hub page directly. It's real physics, no psi content on the page itself: four branches — **Effective Matter** (quasiparticles, effective mass/charge), **Effective Geometry** (emergent-metric systems), **Effective Media** (metamaterials), **Effective Gravity** (analogue-gravity systems — BEC acoustic horizons, Hawking analogues). These are legitimate, and some are experimentally confirmed in the real world (e.g. Steinhauer-style analogue Hawking radiation in Bose-Einstein condensates is real, published physics). The common thread: a real, known microscopic substrate (atoms, a lattice, a fluid) is coarse-grained, and the coarse-grained description mathematically mimics a *different* branch of physics (gravity, particle physics) without any new fundamental ingredient. Nobody has to "prove phonons exist" — they're a mathematical construction on top of atoms everyone already agrees are there.

`Psionic_Engineering`'s hub page self-tags under "Effective Physics" as one of five foundational domains. That categorization is the whole basis for the question being asked here: if psi genuinely worked the way phonons or analogue-gravity horizons do, this would be a real, tractable, test-light research program. So — does it?

## The structural finding: two attempted derivations, both postulate ψ by hand

Checked the two pages that most plausibly attempt to *derive* ψ from something else, rather than just describe its consequences.

**`Effective Field Theory of Consciousness`** (full wikitext read locally, `docs/wiki_cache/`): uses genuine EFT discipline — start from a trusted microscopic model (Wilson-Cowan neural-field theory, a real, well-tested neuroscience model), identify the slow collective mode C(x,t), write the most general Lagrangian consistent with stated symmetries. This is the *correct* way to build an EFT, and the page is honest about its own limits: "The coefficients g₁, …, g₄, g_F, m_C, λ_C are empirically open." But ψ itself enters the Lagrangian as a pre-existing field being coupled to, not as something derived from Wilson-Cowan + electromagnetism. The EFT machinery organizes *how ψ is allowed to couple*, given that it exists — it doesn't establish that it does.

**`5D Action Principle`**: uses real Kaluza-Klein dimensional-reduction machinery (a legitimate, century-old technique — real electromagnetism famously falls out of 5D general relativity this way). But the page's own move is to *postulate* a 5D universe with one extra scalar field and then note that "everything else is mathematics, not new postulates" — which is true of the *consequences*, not of ψ's existence. Ordinary Kaluza-Klein reduction of pure 5D gravity gives you 4D gravity + electromagnetism + one scalar (the "radion") with a fixed physical role tied to the compactification geometry; it doesn't hand you an independent, free-standing ψ field with its own mass and self-coupling. That's added, not derived.

**Same shape, two different toolkits.** In both cases a real, rigorous mathematical apparatus (EFT power-counting; KK reduction) governs *how* ψ interacts once it's assumed — genuinely honest, correctly-done theoretical physics — but ψ's existence itself is postulated in both, not derived from a known substrate the way phonons are derived from atoms. This is worth taking at face value rather than as a knock: it means the wiki is not claiming what "Effective Physics" branch-4 (analogue gravity) claims. It's closer in kind to "we don't know if this field exists, but if it does, here's the disciplined way to describe it" — legitimate EFT practice for a hypothetical field, same genre as beyond-Standard-Model physics (axions, dark photons), not the same genre as phonons.

## The genuinely useful part: the α = 0 sub-theory is real and already testable against existing data

Both pages converge on the same structural fact, stated explicitly in `CEMI_Field_Theory`: setting the ψ-EM coupling constant **α = 0** collapses the whole framework to McFadden's CEMI theory — consciousness identified with the brain's own, already-measured EM field, using "Maxwell electromagnetism only," no new physical entities. CEMI is real (McFadden, *Journal of Consciousness Studies*, 2002–2020), empirically anchored in things that are "real, well-measured, not in dispute" per the wiki's own text: ephaptic coupling (Anastassiou et al., *Nature Neuroscience* 2011), γ-band synchrony and awareness, EEG/MEG/LFP field measurements.

This means the **α = 0 branch of the theory is checkable right now against decades of already-published neuroscience literature** — no new HelmKit hardware, no new pilot session. That's a real, concrete, test-free research task: dig into the existing ephaptic-coupling / CEMI empirical literature and assess how well it actually holds up as a standalone theory of consciousness (separate from anything psi-specific). If it holds up well, the "ordinary" half of the framework is solid ground. If existing data already strains it, that's worth knowing before building anything on top of it.

## The second useful part: the wiki's own analogy points at a real methodology for bounding α without a new experiment

`Effective_Field_Theory_of_Consciousness` states the indirect (ψ-mediated) channel "is diagrammatically identical to the axion-photon vertex... F10 bounds α at axion-class strength." This is a real, meaningful analogy: axion-photon coupling is one of the most heavily constrained hypothetical couplings in physics, and critically, **many of those bounds come from re-analyzing existing precision data** (stellar cooling rates, astrophysical observations, existing precision magnetometry) rather than only from bespoke new detectors. The same move is available here in principle: survey existing ultra-sensitive magnetometry and biomagnetism literature (SQUID noise floors, shielded-room baseline measurements already published for unrelated neuroscience/metrology purposes) for anything that would already constrain α, the way astrophysical data constrains the axion, before any HelmKit-specific measurement. This is a literature-mining task, not a lab task.

## Third useful part: some of the framework's own predictions are checkable against existing public datasets

Three specific, stated predictions in `Effective_Field_Theory_of_Consciousness` don't require a new HelmKit session to get a first read on, because the relevant data types already exist in the public literature:

1. **"Group amplification is N⁴-scaling"** — checkable against existing multi-participant parapsychology meta-analyses (group ganzfeld/PK literature) for scaling-with-group-size trends, reusing the same literature-grounding discipline already applied to the Storm et al. ganzfeld figures.
2. **"Psi performance peaks at the critical/edge-of-trance regime"** — checkable against existing ganzfeld debriefing and state-report literature already compiled in meta-analyses, for a non-monotonic (peaked, not simply "more trance is better") relationship.
3. **"Phase-transition correspondence with Tononi PCI data"** — the perturbational complexity index dataset (Massimini group) is public; whether conscious/unconscious transitions look like an order-parameter phase transition (the specific mathematical claim here) versus some other functional form is a pure data-analysis question on data that already exists.

## The hard ceiling, stated plainly

None of the above can establish that α > 0 — that ψ is *more* than ordinary EM activity. That's not a gap in this audit's effort; it's structural, and the wiki's own axion analogy makes the reason legible: after roughly four decades of dedicated, well-funded axion search physics (haloscopes, helioscopes, light-shining-through-walls), whether the axion-photon coupling is nonzero is *still* an open empirical question. A coupling of that general shape has never been settled by theory or by mining existing data alone in the history of the analogous real search — dedicated, sustained, purpose-built measurement eventually became necessary there, and there's no reason to expect this case is structurally easier. What theory + existing data *can* do is narrow the target, rule out the trivial case if existing evidence already strains it, and make sure any future dedicated measurement is aimed at exactly the right quantity (α, specifically, not a vague "does psi exist") — which is real, non-trivial value, just not a substitute for eventually needing new data if the trivial case survives scrutiny.

## Recommended next step

A literature-reanalysis pass (same discipline as `literature_grounding.md`) on exactly three things: (1) the current empirical standing of CEMI/ephaptic-coupling as a standalone consciousness theory, (2) whether any existing precision-magnetometry/biomagnetism literature already constrains an axion-style α coupling, (3) whether existing meta-analyzed parapsychology/PCI datasets show the N⁴-scaling or critical-regime-peaking signatures the framework predicts. All three use data that already exists; none require a new HelmKit build, pilot, or session.

## Literature pass results (2026-08-06, first cut — web search, not full primary-source reads)

### 1. CEMI/ephaptic coupling — stronger footing than expected, and currently active

Ephaptic-coupling research is not the stale, single-lab 2011 finding it might appear to be — it's an actively growing area. Found a 2026 Frontiers historical review explicitly framing a "contemporary renaissance" in the field, a 2023/2024 eLife mechanistic study (action-potential annihilation inducing electrical coupling between neurons), a 2025 bioRxiv direct empirical validation in printed human neural circuits, a PLOS ONE paper asking directly "Can ephapticity contribute to brain complexity?" (i.e., testing the exact functional-significance question the wiki's CEMI page flags as "the active question"), and a 2023 review proposing a related, mainstream-adjacent "Cytoelectric Coupling Hypothesis" (endogenous fields sculpting neural activity across scales). McFadden's own theory has a 2020 Oxford Academic paper and — most directly useful — a paper titled "A new variant of the electromagnetic field theory of consciousness: approaches to empirical confirmation," which looks like the right thing to read in full next if this thread continues. **Net**: the α = 0 sub-theory rests on ground that's real, current, and still being actively contested/tested by mainstream neuroscience — good news for treating it as the framework's solid floor.

### 2. Axion-style bounds from existing data — the methodology is real, but doesn't transfer numerically as-is

Confirmed the methodology genuinely exists and is standard practice: ABRACADABRA and SHAFT both use SQUID magnetometry to bound axion-photon coupling, a ferromagnet-based search improved bounds using 41 hours of existing-style data, and — the closest match to what was hypothesized — **the SuperMAG Collaboration's existing geomagnetic monitoring network (built for space-weather purposes) has itself been reanalyzed for axion dark-matter signals.** That's a real precedent for "mine an existing sensor network, built for something else, for an exotic coupling bound."

**Important honest caveat, worth stating plainly rather than glossing over**: these bounds are for galactic-halo dark-matter axions in specific mass/frequency windows (SHAFT: 3 kHz–3 MHz Compton frequency, incidentally overlapping the coil's own 1–8 MHz carrier band) with an assumed cosmological source distribution. They do not automatically transfer as a numeric bound on a hypothetical brain-sourced ψ-EM coupling — the source geometry (galactic halo vs. a head-sized biological emitter) is completely different, so the field-strength-to-bound relationship doesn't carry over directly. What transfers is the **method** (reanalyze existing high-sensitivity magnetometry for an anomalous-coupling signature), not the **specific existing number**. A real bound on brain-sourced α would need its own reanalysis with biologically-relevant source assumptions — but the fact that a network built for space weather has already been successfully repurposed this way is a genuine, encouraging existence proof that the approach works in practice, not just in principle.

### 3. N⁴ group-scaling and critical-regime peaking — one caution, one partial lead, one real terminology trap

**N⁴ group-scaling**: did not find anything testing this specific claim. What the ganzfeld meta-analysis literature does show is a **small-study effect** — smaller-N studies report *larger* effect sizes than larger-N studies, the classic signature of publication bias, not a physical scaling law. This is a genuine caution, not a null result to shrug off: naively mining this literature for "does effect size grow with N" would run straight into a confound that looks superficially like scaling but is a bias artifact running in a direction unrelated to (and easily confusable with) the wiki's group-amplification claim. Any real check of N⁴-scaling would need bias-correction methods (trim-and-fill, PET-PEESE) applied first, and even then, "N" here (participants per multi-person psi trial) isn't quite what most ganzfeld meta-analyses vary anyway (they mostly vary study/trial count, not simultaneous-participant count) — this specific prediction may not have adequate existing data to check at all, which is itself worth knowing before spending more effort on it.

**Critical-regime peaking**: partial, genuinely interesting lead. Existing literature does report lower arousal / greater absorption / reduced internal dialogue correlating with better ganzfeld performance, and some (not all) studies report a correlation between attentional-focus/low-arousal state scores and psi performance. But this reads as roughly **monotonic** ("more altered / lower arousal is better") in most of what turned up, not the wiki's specific claim of a **peaked, inverted-U** relationship where too much trance is also bad. That's a real gap between what's claimed and what the existing literature actually shows — worth being precise about rather than rounding "state matters" up to "confirms the critical-regime prediction."

**Terminology trap worth flagging explicitly**: the wiki's prediction cites "Tononi PCI data" — Massimini's Perturbational Complexity Index (TMS-EEG based, clinical/anesthesia consciousness research). The parapsychology literature's altered-state correlate, also abbreviated "PCI," is Pekala's *Phenomenology of Consciousness Inventory* (a self-report questionnaire) — a completely different instrument from a completely different field, sharing only the acronym. Anyone continuing this thread needs to keep these two apart explicitly; conflating them would produce a false sense of convergence between two literatures that aren't actually measuring the same thing.

Sources: [Ganzfeld meta-analysis / small-study effects discussion (Psi Encyclopedia)](https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/meta-analysis-parapsychology/), [Stage 1 Registered Report: Anomalous perception in a Ganzfeld condition (PMC)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7883320/), [Hypnotizability, alterations in consciousness, and other variables as predictors of ganzfeld performance (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/255947455_Hypnotizability_alterations_in_consciousness_and_other_variables_as_predictors_of_performance_in_a_ganzfeld_psi_task), [Testing precognition and altered state of consciousness with selected participants in the Ganzfeld (Edinburgh Research Explorer)](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/testing-precognition-and-altered-state-of-consciousness-with-sele-2/).

## Follow-up pass (2026-08-06, same day): the flagged CEMI paper, and a concrete design for the brain-relevant reanalysis

Two more pieces, closing out the loop this audit opened.

### The flagged "approaches to empirical confirmation" paper — an important finding, but not the one hoped for

Read Strupp's paper on EM field theories of consciousness in full. It does **not** offer a test-free, existing-data path — it's the opposite: Strupp explicitly argues that *all* external-stimulation methods (TMS, tACS, and by direct extension anything a HelmKit coil does) **cannot cleanly test field-causality claims**, because external EM stimulation always simultaneously perturbs both the hypothetical field *and* ordinary neuronal wiring at the same time, with no way to separate which one produced an observed effect. His proposed test instead requires a genuinely new, highly invasive experiment — surgically inserting an electromagnetically-shielding membrane into the interhemispheric fissure during awake neurosurgery, to see whether *decoupling* (not stimulating) the two hemispheres' field communication changes conscious experience despite intact neural wiring. This is obviously outside anything HelmKit could do, and Strupp himself flags it as only opportunistically feasible as an adjunct to existing neurosurgical procedures.

**This is still a genuinely useful finding, just not the one hoped for**: it's a serious, citable methodological critique that applies directly to HelmKit's own architecture, not just to consciousness research generally. Any result from an actively-driven coil — ours or the wiki's — inherits exactly this ambiguity: a measured behavioral or physiological change can't, by this argument, be attributed to "the field" versus "direct EM effects on neural tissue" without some kind of decoupling (not just stimulating) design. This reinforces, from an independent source, the passive/receive-only architecture already flagged as the cleanest option in `mechanism_stress_test_rosters.md` — a receiver can't have this ambiguity because it isn't perturbing anything.

### A concrete (not just analogical) design for the brain-relevant reanalysis

The earlier audit noted that axion-bound methodology is real but doesn't transfer numerically (galactic-halo source geometry, not a human head). Following up: **decades of existing MEG recordings are actually a much better-matched existing dataset than astrophysical axion data**, and don't require inventing anything new to point at. MEG systems in magnetically shielded rooms routinely achieve white-noise sensitivity around 5 fT/√Hz, with modern optically-pumped magnetometers reaching 7–15 fT/√Hz across 1–100 Hz — femtotesla-level sensitivity, recorded from real human heads, for decades, across many labs and public datasets (OpenNeuro, MEG-BIDS repositories, etc.).

**The design**: rather than adapting a galactic-halo axion bound, a dedicated reanalysis would ask whether existing MEG recordings contain any residual field component, beyond what the standard equivalent-current-dipole neural source model predicts, that correlates with the EFT's specific predicted signatures (e.g., the g₁Cψ-type linear source term activating specifically during the "broken phase" / focused-attention states the framework associates with practitioners acting as Jψ sources). This reuses public data, standard MEG forward-modeling tools, and no new hardware.

**The honest bandwidth caveat**: MEG hardware is tuned for the frequency range neural activity actually lives in (roughly DC to a few hundred Hz–1 kHz) — nowhere near the 1–8 MHz carrier band the actual bifilar coil design operates at. So this reanalysis, if run, would test whether the brain's own *passive, ordinary-activity-sourced* ψ-EM coupling (the α term as a background, not as something being actively driven) leaves a detectable low-frequency residual — a real, well-matched, existing-data question. It would say nothing about whether an *actively driven* 1–8 MHz coil does anything, which remains a separate question current MEG archives simply don't cover.

### Net effect of this pass

One piece of the framework (CEMI/ephaptic) comes out stronger than expected — active, current, mainstream-adjacent research, good floor to build on. One piece (axion-bound methodology) is real in principle but doesn't hand over a usable number without a dedicated reanalysis. One piece (N⁴/critical-regime) mostly didn't survive contact with the existing literature as stated — a confound where scaling was hoped for, and a monotonic relationship where a peak was predicted — which is itself useful: it narrows what a future dedicated check would need to establish from scratch rather than assuming the literature already speaks to it.

## Where CEMI/the ψ-EFT actually sits among real, current consciousness theories (2026-08-06)

A broader survey (background research agent) placed CEMI and this framework's ψ-EFT extension against the actual current landscape of mainstream consciousness theories, to calibrate how fringe or mainstream-adjacent this project's theoretical grounding really is — a question this audit hadn't directly asked before.

**The mainstream landscape itself is in real turmoil, not settled orthodoxy to be measured against.** Integrated Information Theory (IIT) — one of the field's two most prominent frameworks — was publicly branded "pseudoscience" in a September 2023 open letter signed by 124 researchers (including Daniel Dennett, Patricia Churchland, and others), over its panpsychism commitment and claims of untestability; IIT's own proponents (Koch, Seth) pushed back publicly. More tellingly: the **COGITATE Consortium**, a large (256-participant, fMRI+MEG+EEG) preregistered adversarial collaboration where both the IIT camp (Tononi) and the Global Workspace Theory camp (Dehaene) co-designed the falsification criteria together, published in *Nature* (April 2025) with a genuinely honest result: **neither theory's core predictions were confirmed**, and both camps publicly accepted the mixed, non-confirmatory outcome rather than declaring victory. This is worth knowing independent of anything psi-related — it means the *entire field* of consciousness science currently lacks a confirmed leading theory, not just the fringe end of it.

**Where CEMI sits in that landscape**: a real, published, credentialed-academic minority position (McFadden, molecular genetics, Surrey; also Susan Pockett's related EM theory) — but one that's largely bypassed by the IIT/GWT/HOT mainstream conversation rather than actively engaged or refuted by it. Not classed as pseudoscience the way psi/parapsychology is; sits below IIT/GWT/HOT in citation density and empirical traction, above Orch-OR in physical plausibility (CEMI needs no exotic quantum-coherence mechanism, just ordinary Maxwell EM — Orch-OR's decoherence-timescale problem, discussed elsewhere in this project's psi grounding, doesn't apply to it).

## Follow-up (2026-08-06, same day): the "relabeled, not refactored" problem, confirmed across five pages

A direct, targeted re-check: does *any* page on the live wiki actually derive ψ from a known physical substrate — real tissue, real quantum system, real material — the way phonons derive from atoms, rather than just using emergence vocabulary around an already-postulated field? Checked five candidates via live browser fetch (not curl): the core `Psi Field` page, `Quantization of the Psi Field`, `Renormalization of Psi Theory`, `Wilson-Cowan Coupled to Psi`, and `Psi Condensation Threshold` (the last surfaced via wiki search specifically because its phase-transition framing looked most likely to be a real derivation attempt).

**All five show the same pattern, and it's a recent one.** `Psi Field` itself (revid 13202, edited 2026-07-23) now opens with real emergent-field rhetoric — "a real, measurable field that emerges from an underlying substrate rather than a fundamental field" — but immediately admits *"the precise functional forms remain an open research problem and are expected to follow from a future microscopic substrate theory."* No substrate is ever named. `Quantization of the Psi Field` and `Renormalization of Psi Theory` (both revid ~8454/8455, 2026-05-18) apply standard QFT machinery to ψ exactly as one would to an already-fundamental field, with the renormalization page's own UV completion tracing straight back to the 5D Action Principle's postulate. `Wilson-Cowan Coupled to Psi` (revid 12585, 2026-07-06) uses "two coupled pendulums" as its own chosen metaphor for the ψ/neural-activity relationship — an explicit admission, in the page's own words, that this is coupling between two independent pre-existing systems, not one emerging from the other. Most tellingly, `Psi Condensation Threshold` (revid 13361, edited **2026-08-05 — one day before this audit**, almost certainly the most recent attempt at exactly this fix) uses genuine phase-transition language (a critical threshold in recurrence/coupling/coherence/integration) but defines that threshold using parameters it then admits, in its own text, it hasn't derived: *"A rigorous version must derive r and φ from the structural parameters rather than treating them as co-equal factors. This derivation is not yet done."*

**Read plainly**: someone attempted, recently, to convert ψ from a postulated field into a genuine effective field — the emergence vocabulary is new and consistent across pages, and the timing (three pages touched within the last three weeks, one the day before this check) is not coincidental. But the vocabulary was added without the underlying derivation — no page names an actual known physical substrate and shows ψ falling out of coarse-graining it, the way `Psi_Field`'s own cited analogues (phonons, magnetization) actually work. Every page defers the real step to future work or states outright it isn't done. This isn't a refactor. It's a relabel that hasn't been backed up yet.

**Adding the ψ-field coupling term moves the combined framework decisively further out.** This project's own ψ-EFT extension of CEMI (the α coupling term, discussed above) inherits none of CEMI's comparative legitimacy — psi/parapsychology is treated by the mainstream consciousness-science conversation as unresolved-at-best, with no channel into the IIT/GWT/HOT/COGITATE-style empirical discourse at all. **Practical calibration, stated precisely rather than left implicit**: "CEMI-as-base-theory" and "CEMI-plus-ψ-coupling" are not the same epistemic bet, and this project's docs should keep treating them as such — the α=0 sub-theory borrows real, if minority, scientific standing; the α≠0 extension does not currently borrow any.
