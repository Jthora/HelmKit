## 0. The one rule

A costume is an object that pretends to do something it does not do. The helm is on camera honestly whenever
**the reason it is on the head is a job it can do at that version, and the claim made on camera never exceeds
the gate that was passed.** Everything below is a way of keeping that rule easy to follow across hundreds of
videos. The persona is a Real Life Super Hero support class: the one who monitors, protects, logs and repairs,
not the one who throws punches. A support class does not need a weapon that works. It needs instruments that tell
the truth. That is precisely what the helm is built to be, so the persona and the engineering pull the same way.

## B. Narrative frames (how to say it so it stays true)

- **B1 Field gear under development.** Every video names the version (print set, firmware phase) and reads the gate table. The helm is always "the current build", never "the finished thing".
- **B2 The support class's instruments.** A medic carries the monitor before anyone needs it; a radio operator wears the headset on quiet days. The helm is worn because the support role is being performed, not because it is finished.
- **B3 Gates and receipts.** Nothing is claimed that a gate has not passed; the gate list from Track M §9 and Track N §5 is on screen; captures and commit hashes are in the description. Null results are published on the same schedule as positive ones.
- **B4 Techno mage as craft discipline.** Rituals are checklists; spells are tested procedures; the grimoire is the repository. The language can be playful. The content stays literal. Psionics 2.0 already made this move on paper: the coil is a CEMI-channel probe, not a psi transducer, until someone measures otherwise.
- **B5 Training loop.** The helm trains the wearer (breathing, recovery) and the wearer trains the helm (calibration, captures, fixtures). Both directions are visible on camera.
- **B6 Investigation ledger.** Cyber investigations are logged with the same discipline: timestamps, integrity, what is unknown. The helm is the lab notebook worn on the head.
- **B7 Open lab.** The STLs, firmware and tools are public; anyone can print the coupon and run the tests. The build case is the openness itself.
- **B8 Showing up.** The support class shows up in the current gear, at the current stage, and says so. That sentence is the whole persona.

## D. Honesty rails (the practicalities that keep it from being for show)

1. **Version card** on screen in every video: print set (vp0.17), firmware phase, the gates passed and the gates open.
2. **Claim ladder.** Four words, used precisely: *measures* (gate passed), *cues* (the pacer, live), *under test* (pre-registered, sham-blind), *housing for* (a part whose contents are not installed or not yet proven). A disk before the coil gates is "housing for the shield and coil elements; foil lining installed; coil under test".
3. **Fixed publication dates** for experiment results, set at pre-registration with `tools/experiment_design.py preregister`, independent of outcome.
4. **Sham-blind on camera.** The counter-wound sham spool is in the design so the wearer cannot know which disk is live. Say so every time the coil comes up.
5. **No medical claims, no stim below Mk1.** The four-belt safety model and the coil gates are part of the story, not hidden from it.
6. **Show the failures.** The lock that could never engage (v0.16), the audit residuals, the bench gate that failed. Failures on camera are what separate a build log from an advertisement.
7. **Receipts in the description:** commit hash, capture file (privacy-scrubbed), analyser output.
8. **Never wear it to impress; wear it because it is doing a job.** If a video has no job for the helm, it stays on the bench in shot.

## E. Support-class duties the helm serves during investigations and research

- **E1 Self-regulation on demand:** prime before a stressful call, recover after; the helm cues it and logs whether it worked.
- **E2 Timestamped ledger:** every session file is a record with integrity counts, the same discipline a case log needs.
- **E3 Evidence with provenance:** POV recording with timestamps and a chain of custody, where lawful; the capture service's index is the custody log.
- **E4 Team support later:** two helms and the dyadic coherence tool make paced breathing a shared exercise; not a claim for today.
- **E5 Endurance:** the combat trim and the forest-run design exist because the support class has to keep going; that is the indefatigability concept, stated as training rather than magic.

## F. Ethical and legal practicalities

- Recording consent laws vary by place; the sensor bar camera is off where consent is not established, and the video says so.
- No impersonation of police or emergency services; the support class de-escalates, gives first aid, logs and calls the people whose job it is.
- No medical advice; the helm is a training and logging instrument.
- Captures contain physiology; scrub or aggregate before publishing; keep raw files private.
- The safety document and the coil gates are public and cited; anything involving current near a head waits for them.

## H. The answer to "does it work?"

"It measures heart rate, breathing and skin response under motion, and logs them with integrity counts. It cues
paced breathing and my round structure. The coil arm is under test with a sham control and publishes on a fixed
date whatever it finds. Here is the gate table. Here is the capture."

That answer is true today, will still be true after a null result, and gets longer only when a gate is passed.
