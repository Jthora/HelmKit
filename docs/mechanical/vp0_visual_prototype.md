## 2. Architecture (v0.3)

Everything hangs off two pods. Each pod is a face-down-printed cup with five **flush 10 mm square sockets** sunk into its skirt, a glued paraboloid reflector, a foam ear ring, and room inside for a 60 × 40 × 8 pouch cell, a 25 mm fan and the power board.

| Port angle | Use | Joined by |
|---|---|---|
| 13° (front, along the brow normal) | brow strut | 39 mm coupler into the wing-end socket |
| 90° (top) | crown arch foot | 23 mm coupler |
| 135° (rear-upper) | spare, antenna pylons | cover |
| 206° (rear, 26° down) | rear band block | 28 mm coupler |
| 300° (front-lower) | strap anchor (chin strap) | direct |

Every socket is locked by one M3 button-head through the host into a thread-formed hole in the plug. Couplers are printed PETG or, preferred, **10 × 10 × 1 mm aluminium square tube** cut to length and drilled 2.5 mm at 7 mm from each end. There are no pivots; to collapse the helm, remove six screws and pull six couplers.

- **Crown arch**: two identical halves, feet with a concave seat on the pod rim, overlap bars with interlocking ridges in the apex sleeve, one M4 clamp gives ±13 mm of span. A 40 × 60 × 12 foam pad under the sleeve carries weight on the crown. No port on top.
- **Brow panel**: 100 mm flat centre with two 60 mm wings swept back 20°, leaning back 13° to follow the forehead. LED window in the bottom face, lid on the head side with a 96 × 44 × 12 foam pad closing the forehead gap. Struts enter sockets in the wing ends.
- **Rear band**: two identical halves in a plane tilted 26°, each starting from a full-height socket block at the pod, hugging the occiput with a 50 × 30 × 12 foam strip, ending in a 66 mm rack. The nape ratchet (10-tooth pinion, sawtooth dial, leaf pawl) pulls both racks; a C bumper guards the dial from below and behind.
- **Retention**: crown pad plus ear cushions carry weight, the rear band bites under the inion, and strap anchors at the front-lower ports take a chin strap. Cheek hooks were dropped as a face-impact hazard.
- **Fillets**: every printed part gets a 1 mm bevel pass on its outer edges; bands use a chamfered section by construction.

Overall width at the pod faces: 232 mm. Widest point: the crown arch feet at about 246 mm.

## 5. Print and validation order

1. Fit coupon: M3 button-head seats in the counterbore, plug slides into the socket block and locks with one screw, pinion runs on the rack, ridge plates interlock.
2. One pod cup and its reflector. Check all five sockets with the coupon's plug, then glue the reflector.
3. Crown halves, apex block, couplers. Check the arch on both pods and the span adjustment.
4. Brow panel, lid, brow couplers. Check the wing sockets align with the front ports with the panel 10 mm off the forehead.
5. Rear band halves, nape parts, rear couplers. Check the racks run in the channels and the dial tightens.
6. Second pod. Foam. Wear it and run.

## 6. Known limitations

- Phantom head has no cheekbones or brow ridge; the brow pad and rear strips are sized from it.
- Ratchet pawl is a 0.9 mm PETG leaf. Expect to move it to TPU or a spring pin. `NAPE_RATCHET_DIR` flips the sawtooth if the dial locks the wrong way.
- Socket lock screws thread directly into printed plugs. Good for a few dozen cycles; heat-set inserts in aluminium couplers are the durable path.
- The header pocket in sockets is gone in v0.3; power to add-ons runs as a pigtail through the vent slots until a connector is chosen.
- Nothing has been drop-tested. Capacities quoted in the review are hand calculations.
